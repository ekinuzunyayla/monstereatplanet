What you'll learn

Names & faces

Use the Feature-Hook method to link a face to a name — so the new colleague, client or neighbor sticks.

Numbers & PINs

Turn digits into pictures with Number-Shape and the Major System. Practice uses random numbers — never your real PINs.

Lists & stories

Chain items into a vivid story with the Link method and recall your shopping list forwards and backwards.

3 minutes a day

Short sessions: a quick review, two or three new hooks, then a recall check. Calm screens, no timers while you learn.

Smart reviews

Spaced repetition brings each item back right before you'd forget it. Reviews are always free.

Built for everyone
