1. Who we are

This policy explains how Ekin Uzunyayla (“we”, “us”) handles information in the game Planet Eater (the “Game”) on Android, iOS and Steam, and on this website.

2. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (planets, monsters, upgrades, gems, Star Tree, album, settings such as language, sound and vibration, and daily counters for rewarded ads) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. We cannot see it or restore it.

  • Save code: Settings lets you export your progress as a text code and import it again. The code contains only game progress and is shared only where you paste it.
  • Delete your data: use Settings → Reset game, clear the app's data, or uninstall the Game.
  • Your operating system may include the app's data in your own device backups (for example Google or iCloud backup), under the terms of those services.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

On mobile, the Game offers optional rewarded ads: a short video you choose to watch to get an in-game bonus. There are no banner or interstitial ads. Ads are not shown in the Steam version.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.). When the ad SDK runs, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (Android advertising ID, or IDFA on iOS if you allow it), app set IDServing, measuring and, if you consent, personalizing ads; frequency capping; fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, fraud prevention, compliance
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Ad delivery and diagnostics
Ad interaction data (ads shown, taps, completion)Rewarding you, measurement, fraud prevention

Ad content is limited to a family-friendly rating (PG). Learn how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the Game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalized. You can change your choice any time with Settings → Privacy options (ads).
  • US states with privacy laws: where required, the same privacy options let you opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information for targeted advertising.
  • iOS: we ask for App Tracking Transparency permission before using the IDFA. If you decline, ads are not personalized.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google settings.
  • You can always simply not watch ads — the Game is fully playable without them.

4. Purchases

If the Game offers paid items (for example a one-time “no ads” purchase), payments are handled by Google Play, the Apple App Store or Steam. We do not receive your card or payment details. The store tells the Game only whether a purchase was successful.

5. Steam

If you play on Steam, Valve processes your account, purchase and, if enabled, achievement and Steam Cloud save data under the Valve Privacy Policy. We may see aggregated sales and achievement statistics in Steamworks.

6. Device permissions

  • Internet / network state: needed only to load ads and the consent form.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback; you can turn it off in Settings.
  • The Game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

7. Support emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and delete them when they are no longer needed (generally within 24 months).

8. Your choices and rights

Because we do not hold personal data about players, most requests are best handled in the Game (Reset game, Privacy options) or with Google, Apple or Valve directly. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the Turkish KVKK, or US state laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object or withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact us and we will help.

Legal bases (where GDPR/KVKK applies): your consent for personalized advertising; our legitimate interest in funding the free Game with non-personalized, optional ads and in preventing ad fraud; and, for support, our legitimate interest in answering you.

9. Children

The Game is designed for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are limited to family-friendly (PG) content. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security and transfers

Your save never leaves your device through us. Google processes ad data in many countries, including the United States, using safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. If we start collecting new kinds of data, we will update this policy before the change ships.
