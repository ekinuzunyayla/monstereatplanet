Delete your account

Memory: Remember Names & Faces · by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Memory app.
  2. Go to Me → Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} with the subject “Delete my Memory account”. Send it from the email address you used to sign in (Apple, Google or email). We may ask you to confirm the request before deleting.

If you never signed in (anonymous account) and have uninstalled the app, we cannot identify your account; its data is deleted automatically after the inactivity period described in our Privacy Policy.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in links
  • Learning progress, reviews, streaks, XP, hearts, gems and settings
  • People I Met names, notes and synced photos
  • Notification tokens

Deletion is completed within 30 days, including backups as they rotate. Purchase records we must keep for tax and accounting may be kept for the period required by law, and aggregated statistics that no longer identify you are not deleted.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play account settings first.
