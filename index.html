Eat the planet.

An idle digging game where cute pixel monsters munch through a whole planet, layer by layer — then fly off to the next one.

What's inside

Hungry monsters

Hire worms, diggers, drills and stranger things. Tap to bite, or let them munch while you're away.

Love & pets

Pet your monsters, find their favorite flavors and fill the Monster Album.

Crust to core

Break zone guardians, find relics and depth events, reach the core — then move to a new planet.

Star Tree & duos

Grow a Star Tree between planets and pair monsters into duos for big team bonuses.

Lost cubs

Spot a cub trapped in amber and tap it in time to rescue it.
