This Privacy Policy explains how {{DEVELOPER_NAME}} (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the mobile app Memory: Remember Names & Faces (the “App”) and this website. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

2.2 Information you give us

  • Sign-in (optional). If you choose to save your progress with Sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive your email address (Apple may give us a private relay address) and a provider identifier. We do not receive your Apple or Google password.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write.

2.3 Purchases

Subscriptions and gem packs are paid through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details. We receive purchase records (product, price, currency, dates, renewal and trial status, transaction IDs) so we can unlock Pro and credit gems.

2.4 Usage, device and diagnostic data

  • Product analytics: in-app events such as “lesson completed” or “paywall viewed”, with app version, platform, language, whether you have Pro, days since install, experiment group and accessibility settings in use (for example, larger text or screen reader on — used only to make sure the App works well for these users). Analytics never include your email or names of people you practice with.
  • Crash and performance data: crash logs, device model, operating system version and app performance metrics.
  • Advertising data (only when you watch a rewarded ad): see section 6.

2.5 What we do not collect

  • We do not perform face recognition or create face geometry, face templates or any other biometric identifiers.
  • We do not ask for or store your real PINs, passwords or card numbers. Number exercises use random numbers.
  • We do not collect precise location, contacts or your photo library. When you add a photo, you pick a single image with the system photo picker.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the App: save and sync your progress, schedule reviews, run hearts, streaks and gems, and unlock purchases.
  • To send reminders you have allowed (review and streak reminders, free-trial-ending reminders). You can turn these off in the App or in your device settings.
  • To understand how the App is used, fix bugs, and test improvements (for example, which lesson order helps people remember more).
  • To show rewarded ads you choose to watch and to verify that you earned the reward.
  • To answer support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not use your learning data or People I Met data for advertising.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Türkiye)

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR or Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies, we rely on:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the App, sync progress and process purchases.
  • Legitimate interests — product analytics, security, fraud prevention and crash reporting, balanced against your rights.
  • Consent — personalized advertising, push notifications and, where required, optional analytics. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — tax and accounting records, responding to lawful requests.

6. Ads, tracking and your choices

  • The App shows only rewarded ads — a video you choose to watch, for example to refill a heart. There are no banner or pop-up ads. Pro members see no ads.
  • When you watch an ad, our ad network may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID / IDFA, if permitted), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information and ad interaction data.
  • iOS: we ask for App Tracking Transparency permission only when you first choose to watch an ad. If you decline, ads are not personalized and the IDFA is not shared.
  • EEA/UK: we ask for consent through a standard consent form (IAB TCF / Google UMP) before ads are personalized.
  • Opt out: use Me → Settings → Privacy → “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” in the App. We also honor the Global Privacy Control signal where required. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

7. People I Met and photos

  • People I Met is a private notebook to help you remember people. By default, “Keep photos on this device only” is on and this data does not leave your device.
  • If you turn on sync (available with a saved account), names, notes and photos are stored in a private storage area that only your account can access. Before upload, photos are resized and location (EXIF) data is removed.
  • Photos are only displayed back to you. We never analyze faces in them.
  • Please only add information about other people that you have a right to keep, and respect their privacy.

8. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your data while your account exists.
  • Delete in the App: Me → Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, progress, People I Met data and photos from our systems within 30 days, including backups as they rotate. You can also request deletion from our website.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription billed by Apple or Google — cancel it in your store account settings.
  • Items you delete in People I Met are removed permanently after 30 days.
  • We may keep limited purchase records as required for tax and accounting, and aggregated or de-identified statistics that no longer identify you.
  • If you never create a saved account and uninstall the App, your anonymous account cannot be recovered. Inactive anonymous accounts may be deleted after {{INACTIVE_PERIOD}} of inactivity.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Database access rules ensure each account can only read and write its own data. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

10. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, and for Türkiye, the transfer mechanisms set out in the KVKK.
